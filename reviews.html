FAQ

Do you take walk-ins?
Yes — walk-ins are always welcome, though booking ahead helps guarantee your preferred time.
What forms of payment do you accept?
Cash, all major cards, and contactless payment.
Can I book with a specific person?
Yes — just mention your preference when you call or book.
What's your cancellation policy?
We ask for at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule.
